FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding DLT registration, messaging protocols, and APIs.

DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) registration is a mandatory blockchain system mandated by TRAI in India to prevent spam. Every business sending commercial SMS must register their Entity, Sender IDs (Headers), and SMS Templates on telecom operator DLT portals.

RCS (Rich Communication Services) upgrades standard SMS with rich media carousels, action buttons, verified brand profiles, and interactive replies directly inside the native Android Messages app.

WhatsApp Business API allows medium-to-large enterprises to automate customer notifications, send OTPs, manage multi-agent support inboxes, and run broadcast campaigns through official Meta API endpoints.

SMPP (Short Message Peer-to-Peer) is an industry-standard TCP protocol designed for high-volume SMS dispatches. It provides persistent connection sessions with high TPS throughput for telecom aggregators.

SMS Fallback automatically dispatches a standard SMS message if an RCS or WhatsApp message fails to deliver to recipient devices due to offline data connectivity, ensuring 100% reach.

Transactional SMS are critical alerts, OTPs, and updates sent 24x7 to registered users. Promotional SMS are marketing offers and dispatches sent between TRAI-regulated hours (9 AM to 9 PM) to opted-in users.
